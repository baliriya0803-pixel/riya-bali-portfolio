About

Design that's meant to be scrolled past twice.

I'm a second-year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Toronto (PEY Co-op) — but most of my design instincts come from three years of running social accounts and building brand systems for organizations that couldn't afford an agency.

I've designed reels that outperformed follower baselines, launched a nonprofit's entire digital presence from a blank canvas, and kept a 7-person team's templates on-brand. I like projects where a good graphic has to do real work: raise a donation number, grow an account, or make a cause legible to someone scrolling fast.

Currently: Publicity Director at the Engineering Athletics Association and Graphic Designer at the Sustainable Engineers Association.

I also post my personal art on Instagram — @eupho.riyaaa.
